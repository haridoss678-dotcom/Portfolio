Personal Project

Local Multi-Modal RAG Assistant

A conversational RAG system that indexes PDFs (digital, scanned, and legacy Tamil-font), audio, video, and YouTube links, then answers follow-up-aware questions in English, Tamil, or Tanglish — with a real page number or audio timestamp cited on every factual sentence, and an explicit “not found” instead of a guess when the corpus doesn’t cover the question.
